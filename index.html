Good to know

Before you call

Do I need to stock it myself?

Yes — you (or your staff) load and restock the machine. The companion app tells you exactly what's low so restocking is quick.

What's covered under warranty?

Every machine includes a 1-year warranty. If a part fails within that year, it's replaced at no charge for the part.

What if a customer's card doesn't work or a part fails?

Call Emmanuel above. He'll troubleshoot it with you and bring in a technical specialist if the machine needs one.

What can it sell, and where can it go?

Dry goods only — snacks, chips, shelf-stable items. It plugs into a standard outlet. It sits on a tabletop or counter — just make sure the surface is rated to hold at least 120 lb. This machine is meant for indoor placement only; do not place it outdoors where it can get rained on or take prolonged sun exposure.

Can it sell age-restricted products?

Yes, the built-in scanner supports age/ID verification for restricted items in addition to standard snacks.
